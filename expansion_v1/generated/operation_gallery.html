Two blue cards, two red cards, or one of each may be used together. Diagram colors distinguish mathematical roles, not card types. Each matrix has 2–4 rows and 2–4 columns; use exact arithmetic. Print at 100% on A4 with background graphics enabled.

OPERATIONO01

Add a Matrix

Add the corresponding entries of two matrices. Both matrices must have the same number of rows and columns.

OPERATIONO65

Clear One Entry

Choose one entry and set it to 0. Every other entry stays unchanged.

OPERATIONO11

Right Multiply

Multiply A by B on the right. The number of columns in A must equal the number of rows in B.

OPERATIONO21

Append a Row or Column

Append a whole row from B below A, or a whole column from B to the right of A. Its length must match an existing row or column of A, respectively.

OPERATIONO73

Add or Subtract Rows / Columns

Choose two distinct rows or two distinct columns of one matrix. Add or subtract the source from the target. Use one sign for the whole vector; keep the source unchanged.

OPERATIONO27

Transpose

Turn every row into a column. An m × n matrix becomes n × m.

OPERATIONO30

Interchange

Exchange two existing rows or two existing columns.

OPERATIONO06

Subtract a Matrix

Subtract each entry of B from the corresponding entry of A. A and B must have the same number of rows and columns.

OPERATIONO16

Add to a Row or Column

Add a whole row or column from B to a row or column of A with the same length and orientation.

OPERATIONO25

Replace a Row or Column

Replace a row or column of A with a whole row or column from B of the same length and orientation.

OPERATIONO33

+1 to Every Entry

Add exactly 1 to every entry, including entries outside the diagonal. J is the all-ones matrix.

OPERATIONO35

−1 from Every Entry

Subtract exactly 1 from every entry, including entries outside the diagonal. J is the all-ones matrix.

OPERATIONO38

Multiply by 2

Double every entry.

OPERATIONO37

Multiply by −1

Reverse the sign of every entry.

OPERATIONO39

Inverse

Find the inverse of a square, invertible matrix.

OPERATIONO40

LU Factorization

For a square matrix, use Doolittle LU with unit diagonal in L and no row swaps. The first n − 1 pivots must be nonzero. Record either L or U.

OPERATIONO41

±1 to One Entry

Choose one entry and either add 1 or subtract 1. Every other entry stays unchanged.
